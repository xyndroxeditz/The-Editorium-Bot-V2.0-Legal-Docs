# Privacy Policy - Editorium Bot

**Last Updated: October 18, 2025**

## 1. Introduction

Editorium Bot ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Discord bot.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information Provided by Discord
- Discord User ID, username, and discriminator
- Server/guild membership information
- Profile information (avatar, status, roles)

### 2.2 Information You Provide
- Profile information (bio, specialty, software skills, portfolio links)
- Job postings and applications
- Social media links (for premium users)
- Communication history and messages
- Payment information for premium subscriptions

### 2.3 Automatically Collected Information
- Usage statistics and interaction data
- Command usage patterns
- XP and gamification progress
- Job application and completion statistics
- Premium subscription status and history

### 2.4 Information from Third Parties
- Payment processor information (for premium subscriptions)
- Discord API data

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Service Provision
- To provide and maintain the Bot's features
- To process job postings and applications
- To manage premium subscriptions
- To facilitate user interactions and communications

### 3.2 Community Features
- To display profiles and portfolios
- To enable job matching and applications
- To maintain leaderboards and gamification
- To provide social features and networking

### 3.3 Communication
- To send service-related notifications
- To respond to support requests
- To send premium-related communications
- To notify about updates and changes

### 3.4 Analytics and Improvement
- To analyze usage patterns and improve features
- To monitor service performance and reliability
- To develop new features and services
- To generate anonymized statistics

### 3.5 Legal Compliance
- To comply with legal obligations
- To enforce our Terms of Service
- To protect rights and safety

## 4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

### 4.1 Public Information
- Profile information is publicly visible within Discord servers
- Job postings and applications may be visible to other users
- Leaderboard and achievement data is public

### 4.2 Service Providers
- We may share information with service providers who assist our operations
- Payment processors for premium subscriptions
- Hosting and database providers
- Analytics services (anonymized data only)

### 4.3 Legal Requirements
- We may disclose information if required by law
- To respond to legal process or government requests
- To protect our rights or the safety of others
- To investigate violations of our Terms

### 4.4 Business Transfers
- In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets
- Information may be transferred as part of the transaction
- Users will be notified of any such changes

## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Security Measures
- Data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- Access is limited to authorized personnel
- Regular security audits and updates
- Secure database infrastructure

### 5.2 Data Retention
- Active user data is retained while accounts are active
- Premium subscription data retained for tax and accounting purposes
- Some anonymized data retained indefinitely for analytics
- Data deleted upon account deletion or legal requirements

### 5.3 Data Breaches
- We will notify affected users of any security breaches
- Notifications will be sent via Discord and email (if available)
- We will take appropriate remedial measures

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

### 6.1 Access and Control
- You can view your profile and data through Bot commands
- You can update your information using profile commands
- You can delete your account and associated data

### 6.2 Data Portability
- You can request a copy of your data
- Data will be provided in a structured, machine-readable format
- Contact support for data export requests

### 6.3 Data Deletion
- You can delete your profile using `/delete_profile`
- Premium data may be retained for legal/financial reasons
- Some anonymized data cannot be deleted

### 6.4 Opt-out Options
- You can opt out of non-essential communications
- Premium notifications can be managed through settings
- Marketing communications are minimal and opt-out available

## 7. Cookies and Tracking

### 7.1 Discord Integration
- The Bot operates within Discord's infrastructure
- Discord's own cookies and tracking policies apply
- We do not use additional tracking cookies

### 7.2 Analytics
- We may use anonymized analytics to improve the service
- No personally identifiable information is used for analytics
- Analytics data is aggregated and cannot be traced to individuals
